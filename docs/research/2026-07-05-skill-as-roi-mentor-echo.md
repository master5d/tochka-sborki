# Skill-as-ROI packaging — de-hustled framework for mentor + Echo

> Derived from the IndigoGo competitor dossier (`2026-07-04-indigogo-competitor-dossier.md`, §4).
> This is an **analytical framework + recommendations**, not finished sales copy. It gives the
> structure and the authenticity guardrails; the final owner-voice landing copy stays with the owner.
> Ticket: `fb_8adc4e04`.

## 1. The extracted pattern (what IndigoGo's B2B does)

IndigoGo's «речь-под-продажи» B2B offer runs a clean **skill-as-ROI** play. Stripped of the
brand, the reusable shape is four parts:

1. **Segment → concrete promise.** One capability, sold to distinct roles, each with an outcome
   in that role's own language (sales: conversion/deal-size; leaders: sharper presentations,
   shorter meetings; support: faster de-escalation, fewer escalations).
2. **Diagnostic → training → application arc.** Week 1 individual diagnosis → weeks 2–7 daily
   practice + weekly group sessions → week 8 applied on real scenarios. The learner never just
   "takes a course" — they diagnose, practice, then apply in real work.
3. **A named, time-anchored container.** "56 days / 8 weeks", "first results in 21 days",
   "180 days of transformation". A concrete arc, not an open-ended subscription.
4. **Pricing/entry hooks.** Free 48h express team audit · −50% for the first 10 companies ·
   50/50 no-interest installments.

Parts **1–3 are structurally sound and worth adapting.** Part 4 is where the manipulation lives
(see §4 below) — take the *free audit* idea, reject the rest.

## 2. De-hustled adaptation — mentor.mamaev.coach (B2B agent-engineering)

**The capability:** a team learns to co-work with AI agents (vibe coding / agent engineering) —
the same craft the Точка Сборки course teaches individuals, delivered to a team as a business
capability.

**Honest outcome framing (the key de-hustle).** Do NOT promise fabricated percentage lifts
("+25–40% conversion"). Frame the outcome as *what the team will be able to DO*, verifiable by
the team's own eyes:

- *"After this, your team can take a real internal task and build a working automation for it
  end-to-end — you'll have shipped at least one."*
- *"Your people stop waiting on a backlog and start delegating the mechanical half of their work
  to an agent, keeping judgment and client contact human."*

Outcomes are **demonstrated, not claimed** — the deliverable is a thing the team built, not a
number in a slide.

**Structure (adapt the diagnostic→practice→application arc):**
1. **Diagnosis** — a short session mapping the team's real repetitive tasks and where an agent
   fits (mirrors the shipped `/exercises` "automate your practice" + SOP-track thinking).
2. **Practice** — the course's own loop (intent → systems → design → step) run on the team's
   real tasks, not toy examples.
3. **Application** — each person ships one small automation in their actual workflow; the arc
   ends with working artifacts, not a certificate alone.

**Offer shape (honest):** a named, time-boxed engagement (e.g. a 4–6 week team arc) with a clear
before/after in the team's own words. Entry = a genuine free diagnostic call (the useful part of
IndigoGo's "free audit"), with NO countdown and NO "first N companies" scarcity.

**Reuses the anti-dependency ethic:** the goal is a team that no longer needs the mentor —
graduation, not retention (mirrors `mentor-persona.ts` + the anti-dependency review lens).

## 3. De-hustled adaptation — Echo Meeting Copilot (go-to-market)

**The ROI is real and demonstrable, so it needs no inflation.** IndigoGo's whole B2B loop depends
on *manual* feedback — human trainers recording video reviews on a 6-month cycle. Echo's delta is
exactly that gap: **sovereign AI in the loop** — local STT + TTS + instant pronunciation/delivery
analysis on the person's own real recording.

**Frame the ROI as the delta, honestly:**

- *"They analyze your speech by hand, on a delay. Echo does it on your real call or meeting,
  instantly, on your own machine."*
- The B2B outcome is **real call/meeting analysis** (delivery, pace, clarity) as concrete
  feedback — not self-assessment, not a trainer's delayed video.

**What makes it honest + on-brand:** the ROI claim is *the mechanism*, which is verifiable by
running it once — not a fabricated conversion metric. Echo's sovereignty (local, private, the
user's own data) is itself the trust signal, replacing IndigoGo's "256-bit SSL" trust-theater.

**Go-to-market shape:** offer a real run on the buyer's own recording as the "diagnostic" (the
honest version of the free audit). Let the mechanism sell itself; do not attach urgency.

## 4. What to REJECT (the anti-patterns)

These are the parts of the IndigoGo playbook that violate the mamaev.coach authenticity boundary —
copy the structure, never these:

- **Fake scarcity** — "−50% for the first 10 companies", countdowns, "N spots left". The brand
  is sole-prop and authenticity-sacred; no manufactured urgency.
- **Unverifiable / inflated metrics** — "+25–40% conversion", "NPS +40–60", "95% completion",
  "75 000+ students". If a number can't be shown to *this* buyer honestly, don't use it. Prefer
  demonstrated outcomes (a thing built, a mechanism run) over claimed percentages.
- **Shopfront thicker than the substance** — the dossier (§5) caught IndigoGo's `/premium` and
  `/calendar` rendering empty: marketing promising more than the product delivers. Never ship a
  page that over-promises; show the real thing (a live diagnostic, a real Echo run) instead.
- **Vanity social proof** — before/after audio of named students, star-count walls. Use real,
  earned proof only (the shipped showcase real/dream split already encodes this).
- **NEVER nonprofit / tax-deductible framing** — sole proprietor; this is a business capability
  sold by the maker.

## 5. One-line takeaways

- **mentor B2B:** sell a *demonstrated capability* (the team ships something), not a promised percentage.
- **Echo:** sell the *mechanism* (instant sovereign analysis) — it's real, so it needs no inflation.
- **Both:** keep IndigoGo's diagnostic→practice→application arc and its "free diagnostic" entry;
  drop its scarcity, its inflated metrics, and its shopfront-over-substance.
